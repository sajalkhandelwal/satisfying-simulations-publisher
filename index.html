Satisfying Simulations Local Publisher helps its owner prepare original short simulation videos, review the exact video and platform copy, and deliver approved work to the owner's selected YouTube channel.

What the app does

  1. Creates local video masters and proposed titles, descriptions and tags.
  2. Connects through Google's consent flow and checks that the authorized YouTube channel exactly matches the channel selected by the owner.
  3. Holds each exact master and its metadata for owner approval. A changed master or changed metadata requires a fresh review.
  4. After approval and when publishing is enabled, uploads the video privately, checks processing, and applies the approved visibility setting. Delivery IDs and status records support recovery and help avoid duplicate uploads.
  5. Can read statistics for known uploaded videos. Optional YouTube Analytics reports require an additional explicit permission.

Local operation

The publisher runs on the owner's computer, with a local review desk, SQLite records and local media artifacts. The current setup uses a local Ollama model to propose metadata; no hosted AI fallback is configured. Model output still needs owner review.

This is a personal tool, not a public account-registration service. This website does not perform Google sign-in or collect credentials. Google authorization takes place separately for the desktop app.

Privacy and control

Read the privacy disclosure for the Google data accessed, local storage, optional metrics, retention and removal of access. The owner controls whether to connect, approve or enable publishing.
